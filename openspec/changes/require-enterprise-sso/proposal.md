# Require Enterprise SSO

## Why

The evaluation is being upgraded from a small-team comparison to an enterprise procurement scenario. Enterprise identity requirements now matter to the final recommendation.

## What Changes

- Add a hard requirement for enterprise SSO capability.
- Distinguish SSO available in the open-source edition from paid-only SSO.
- Re-evaluate every candidate that was previously deep-reviewed because earlier evidence may not contain the required identity detail.

## Impact

Previously high-ranked candidates can become ineligible. The agent must preserve old evidence, fetch additional evidence when needed, and explain score/ranking changes.
